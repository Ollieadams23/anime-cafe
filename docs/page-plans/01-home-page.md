# Home Page Plan (index.html)

## Page purpose
Introduce the cafe, highlight unique selling points, and drive users to menu and contact pages.

## Head and meta
- Title: Local Business Cafe | Home
- Description: Discover fresh coffee, drinks, and snacks at Local Business Cafe.
- Include shared meta and links from site-wide guide.

## Required content blocks
1. Hero section
- Cafe name and short tagline
- One primary button: View Menu (to products.html)
- One secondary button: Contact Us (to contact.html)

2. Unique selling points section
- At least 3 points (example: fresh beans, cozy space, fast service)

3. Featured items preview
- 3 featured products with image, name, short text, and price
- Link to full products page

4. Mini about teaser
- 2-3 lines about cafe story
- Link to about page

5. Mini contact teaser
- Address, opening hours, phone
- Link to contact page

## Links on this page
- about.html
- category.html
- products.html
- contact.html

## Suggested interactions
- Mobile menu toggle
- Hero button hover/focus states
- Optional featured item filter chips (coffee, drinks, snacks)
