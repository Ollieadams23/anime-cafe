# About Page Plan (about.html)

## Page purpose
Explain the cafe story, mission, and values so users trust the brand.

## Head and meta
- Title: Local Business Cafe | About
- Description: Learn about Local Business Cafe, our story, values, and what makes us special.
- Include shared meta and links from site-wide guide.

## Required content blocks
1. About hero
- Page title and one-line mission

2. Story section
- How the cafe started
- Why the cafe exists

3. Values section
- 3 values (quality, community, consistency for example)

4. Team or founder section
- Short bio and photo (or illustration)

5. Why choose us section
- Quick bullet points tied to customer value

## Links on this page
- index.html
- category.html
- products.html
- contact.html

## Suggested interactions
- Simple tab or accordion for story, mission, values (optional)
- Hover styles on value cards
