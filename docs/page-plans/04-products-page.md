# Products Page Plan (products.html)

## Page purpose
Display products with image, description, and price in a clean, scannable format.

## Head and meta
- Title: Local Business Cafe | Products
- Description: Explore our coffee, drinks, and snacks with prices and descriptions.
- Include shared meta and links from site-wide guide.

## Required content blocks
1. Products hero
- Title and short summary

2. Product groups
- Coffee group
- Drinks group
- Snacks group

3. Product card content
Each card includes:
- Product image
- Product name
- Short description
- Price

4. Optional notice area
- Allergy note or seasonal availability

## Links on this page
- index.html
- about.html
- category.html
- contact.html

## Suggested interactions
- Category filter buttons (all, coffee, drinks, snacks)
- Sort by price (optional)
- Active filter feedback
