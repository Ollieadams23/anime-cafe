# Contact Page Plan (contact.html)

## Page purpose
Provide contact details and allow users to send a message.

## Head and meta
- Title: Local Business Cafe | Contact
- Description: Contact Local Business Cafe for inquiries, bookings, and feedback.
- Include shared meta and links from site-wide guide.

## Required content blocks
1. Contact intro
- H1 and short welcome line

2. Contact information
- Phone
- Email
- Address
- Opening hours

3. Contact form (recommended)
Fields:
- Name
- Email
- Message

4. Location section
- Embedded map or map placeholder image with address text

## Links on this page
- index.html
- about.html
- category.html
- products.html

## Suggested interactions
- Form validation messages (required fields, valid email)
- Success feedback after submit simulation
